# Solaris Software {#mainpage}

## Welcome to Solaris Software!

We're glad you're here.

At Solaris Software we believe that software should not be a limitation for a
team to compete. That is why our software is free to use under a
[CC BY-NC-SA Creative Commons license](https://creativecommons.org/cc-licenses/).
What this means:

- Credit must be given to Solaris (UVigo Aerotech), the creator.
- Only non-commercial use is permitted. Non-commercial means not primarily
  intended for or directed towards commercial advantage or monetary
  compensation. We do this to keep our software from being used for military
  or business advantage &mdash; we want our code to be free to use and free of
  charge without causing harm to others.
- Adaptations must be shared under the same terms.

We want teams and people around the globe to collaborate on our project. Join
us to make better software.
